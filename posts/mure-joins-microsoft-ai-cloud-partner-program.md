## Mure joins the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program

We’re excited to announce that Mure has officially joined the [Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program](https://www.linkedin.com/showcase/microsoft-cloud-partner-program/)!

This isn't a badge. It's a reflection of where we're going.

Our team has spent years building intelligent data systems on Azure and the Microsoft data stack for large enterprises as well as SMBs. We didn't study the stack from the outside. We shipped things on it, learned what actually works, and built tools. That experience shaped how we think about data engineering and it's the foundation of what we do at Mure Data.

By aligning with Microsoft, Mure now operates as an AI data engineering firm supporting Microsoft Fabric, Foundry, and the broader Microsoft data and AI stack.

This partnership marks another step in our [agency](/#services) mission to build agentic data systems. Stronger alignment, same skin in the game, same ambition.

More to come.
